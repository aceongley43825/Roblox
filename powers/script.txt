In this town, every kid gets a superpower on their tenth birthday. My name is Jay, and today I turned ten. My sister got super speed. My best friend Noah got super strength. I waited all day. Nothing happened. Everyone laughed and called me Powerless Jay. That night, I sat alone in the park and wished for any power at all. Suddenly, every light in town went out. People started screaming. Noah tried to fix the power plant, but he just ripped the door off. My sister ran so fast she crashed into a tree. Then I noticed something. Wherever I walked, the lamps turned back on. I ran to the power plant and put my hands on the generator. The whole town lit up brighter than ever. Everyone cheered my name. The mayor gave me a medal and said, Your power is light. I smiled. Then I sneezed. And every light in town exploded.
