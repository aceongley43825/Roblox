In my town, there's a vending machine that grants any wish. It only costs one Robux. But every wish takes something away. My name is Sam, and I had exactly one Robux left. My best friend Mia told me, Don't do it. But I was tired of being invisible. So I put in my last Robux and wished to be popular. The next morning, everyone at school knew my name. Kids gave me gifts. They followed me everywhere. Then I saw Mia in the hallway. I waved at her. She just looked at me and said, Sorry, do I know you? My heart dropped. The machine took my best friend. I ran back to the machine and banged on the glass. I don't want to be popular! I want my friend back! The screen flashed, Wish undone. Suddenly, everyone forgot me again. Except Mia. She ran up and hugged me. Then she pulled out a tiny remote. She pressed a button, and the vending machine lit up. She smiled and said, I built it. I just wanted to see if you'd choose me.
