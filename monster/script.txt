In this town, every hundred years, a giant monster rises from beneath the ground. But what nobody knows is that the monster isn't hunting people randomly. It's coming to collect a debt. // For generations, everyone has followed one rule. When the sirens go off, you have ten minutes to get underground. If you're caught outside, you're never seen again. This year was the hundredth anniversary. At exactly midnight, the sirens started blaring. I ran into my basement and turned on the news. The mayor appeared on every channel. He said, "Whatever happens tonight, do not leave your homes." But then the broadcast suddenly changed. A security camera showed the monster walking straight toward city hall. I watched as it smashed through the entrance and disappeared underground. Then my phone rang. It was the mayor, my grandpa. He was crying. He told me that a hundred years ago, the town made a deal with the monster. Every century, the mayor must sacrifice ten people to keep it asleep. But this year, he refused to sacrifice anyone. That's why the monster had returned. Suddenly, another emergency broadcast appeared. It showed my grandpa standing in front of the camera. He looked directly into it and said, "There's something I never told you. The monster isn't coming to destroy our town." He paused. /// "It's coming to take back the ten people we stole from it." Then the camera turned to the room behind him. And standing there were ten people who had disappeared exactly a hundred years ago. They hadn't aged a single day. ///
