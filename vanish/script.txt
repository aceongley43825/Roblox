In this town, every night at exactly midnight, one house disappears. Nobody knows why, or where they go. But there's one rule everybody follows. If your house is chosen, never look outside. My name is Jake. I've lived here my whole life. Next door lives Linda, and her pink flamingo. Across the street, my best friend Kevin. One night, at midnight, Then I heard a scream. // From Linda's house. I knew the rule. But I looked anyway. Her lights flickered. The roof lifted off. The whole house floated into the sky. Gone. No trace. The next morning, only grass. But something was wrong. Kevin walked right past. I asked, where's Linda's house? He said, who's Linda? Mom said that lot was always empty. Nobody remembered her. Nobody but me. Then I saw her pink flamingo, standing in my yard, staring at my window. The houses aren't chosen. They're taken when someone looks. The scream was bait. The next night, I heard another scream. From my own front yard. /// So I didn't look. But across the street, Kevin did. Never look outside.
