In this town, everyone has a countdown above their head. Nobody knows where they came from, but everyone knows what happens when yours reaches zero. You disappear. My countdown said eighteen years, so I wasn't worried. But one morning, something strange happened. Everyone's countdown went down by one day, like always. But mine went UP by ten years. I thought it was a glitch, until I noticed something. Nobody else could see my countdown anymore. I ran to the mayor's office and demanded answers. He looked at me and went completely silent. Then he locked the doors. He told me the countdowns weren't counting down to our deaths. They were counting down to the day we were supposed to disappear. I asked him why mine was going up. He looked terrified. Then he whispered, "Because you're not supposed to disappear." Suddenly, every countdown in town changed to zero. Everyone vanished. Everyone except me, and the mayor. Then my phone rang. It was the mayor calling. But he was standing right in front of me, and his phone was still in his pocket. The voice on the phone whispered, "Don't turn around."
