In this town, everybody parties. Except me. I fix arcade machines. HUSS! HUSS! Pool parties. Pizza parties. A graduation party for the mayor's goldfish. Nobody here plays games. Why would they? Best friend Milo dances on tables. Neighbor Rita throws pool parties. Even Grandma does the limbo. But not me. Day by day, HUSS! I fixed them. HUSS! I beat them all. HUSS! Every high score: mine. Then came the party of the year. And one arcade machine. Everybody laughed. Party King Derek: who's the beanie guy? I grabbed the stick. HUSS! HUSS! HUSS! Fifty thousand. One million. All by myself. The music stopped. The crowd went silent. When did you get so good? The truth? While you were partying. The next night, something was wrong. Empty pool. Empty chairs. Then, from every window, HUSS! Milo was gaming. Rita bought three machines. Grandma was speed-running pinball. Nobody parties anymore. The whole town is gaming. Which is great, except I'm alone on Fridays. So I threw a party. The only guest: the mayor's goldfish. He beat my high score. HUSS!
