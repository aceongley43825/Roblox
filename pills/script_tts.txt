In this town, everyone who turns eighteen goes to the town square, and picks one of two pills. The gold pill makes you instantly rich. Nobody knows what the black pill does. Nobody ever picks it. Why would they? My best friend Theo picked gold last year. Now he owns a gold car, and his hamster has a mansion. This year, I turn eighteen. But there's one problem. My little sister Mia is very sick. Her medicine costs one million coins. We have twelve. My dad keeps saying, take the gold pill. It fixes everything. Get rich. Buy the medicine. Save Mia. Then came Choosing Day. The whole town showed up. First kid. Gold. Instantly rich. Next kid. Gold. Next. Gold. Then it was my turn. I reached for the gold pill. Then I saw him. Old Mister Pickles, the pharmacist. The only man who ever picked black. He shook his head. So I took the black pill. The crowd went silent. Nothing happened. No money. No sparkles. Everybody laughed. Even Theo. My dad just stared at me. That night, Mia got worse. I held her hand. And my hand started glowing. Then Mia sat up. Her cheeks went pink. She said, I'm hungry. The black pill doesn't make you rich. It makes you a healer. By morning, the whole town knew. There was a line outside our house. Rich people get sick too. And they pay in gold. Now we're the richest family in town. Which is great, except my first patient every morning is Theo's hamster. He has a cold. Again. And Dad? He still keeps saying it. The black pill fixes everything.
