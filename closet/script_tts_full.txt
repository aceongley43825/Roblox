In this town, everybody tapes their closet shut every night. Big silver X. Except me. HUSS! HUSS! Closets taped. Fridges taped. Even the mayor's goldfish bowl. Nobody goes out after dark. Why would they? My best friend Dex tapes his twice. Neighbor Rhonda uses three rolls. Grandma sleeps holding the tape. But not me. My ball lives in there. Day by day, HUSS! night by night, HUSS! Then came the big game. Everybody showed up, yawning. I walked on. Everybody laughed. Who's the kid in the beanie? I got the ball. HUSS! HUSS! Fifty points. All by myself. The crowd went silent. When did you get so good? I told him: while you were taping, I was practicing. The next night, something was wrong. Closets open. Tape in the trash. Then, from every window: HUSS! HUSS! Dex, dribbling. Rhonda, karate. Grandma, push-ups. Now the whole town hustles all night. Which is great... except the court is always full. So I taped my closet shut. Then something knocked. It was the mayor's goldfish. He's actually great at defense. HUSS!
