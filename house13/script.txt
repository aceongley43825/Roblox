In this town, there's one house that nobody is allowed to enter. House Number 13. Mayor Grimley tells everyone it's cursed. He says anyone who goes inside will disappear forever. So every kid walks past it a little faster, and every grown-up crosses the street. But I always wondered why a mayor would care so much about one old house. So one night, I climbed out of my bedroom window and walked straight to House Number 13. The front door wasn't even locked. Inside, everything was covered in dust. The stairs, the sofa, the paintings on the walls. Everything except one room at the end of the hall. That room was spotless. The walls were covered with dozens of newspaper articles, all about Mayor Hollis, the old mayor who mysteriously disappeared twenty years ago, on the same night Grimley became mayor. And on the desk, there was a cup of tea. It was still warm. // Then I heard footsteps behind me. An old man was standing in the doorway, holding a candle. He said, "Please don't go outside. The town is cursed." It was Mayor Hollis. Grimley had told him that anyone who stepped outside would disappear forever. So for twenty years, he stayed inside, and the whole town stayed out. Hollis and the whole town were scared of the exact same lie. So I took his hand, opened the front door, and walked him into the street. Nothing happened. The next morning, the whole town came to see him. Grimley took one look at us and ran so fast he left a shoe behind. Nobody has seen him since. Some people say he disappeared forever. ///
