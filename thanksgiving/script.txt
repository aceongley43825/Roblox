In this town, the turkeys cook Thanksgiving dinner, not the people. Every year, the biggest turkey in town, Mayor Gobbles, walks door to door with a clipboard and asks every family one question. What are you thankful for? If he likes your answer, you're invited to his feast. If he doesn't, you're on the menu. // My name is Max, and I was never scared, because my family always just says "each other." But this year, when Gobbles knocked on our door, my dad was watching football, and he yelled, "I'm thankful for turkey sandwiches!" // Gobbles slowly wrote something on his clipboard. Then he looked at me. I panicked and said the first thing in my head. "I'm thankful for you, Mayor Gobbles." He stared at me for a really long time. Then his eyes filled with tears, and he hugged me. That night, my mom, my sister and I ate the best dinner of our lives at his feast. Everything was perfect, except for one thing. The main course was sitting in a giant pie dish, waving at me. It was my dad. ///
