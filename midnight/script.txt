In this town, every night at midnight, someone gets replaced. Nobody knows who will disappear, or who will take their place. That's why everyone locks their doors at eleven fifty-nine. My name is Jake, and I never believed the rumors. My best friend Leo did. He said the copies always get one tiny thing wrong. Until one night, I woke up to screaming downstairs. I ran down and saw my mom standing in the kitchen. But something was wrong. She wasn't moving. She was just staring at me. I asked, Mom, are you okay? She smiled and said, Of course, sweetie. My mom never calls me sweetie. She calls me buddy. Then I heard screaming again. It was coming from the basement. I opened the door, and there was my real mom, tied to a chair. She whispered, Jake, run. I turned around. The other mom was right behind me. Still smiling. And that's when I had an idea. I said, Mom, what's my favorite food? She froze. Her face started glitching. Pizza, she said. Wrong. It's tacos. Suddenly she flickered, sparkled, and disappeared. I untied my real mom, and she hugged me. The next morning, I told Leo everything. He just stared at me. Then he smiled and said, I believe you, sweetie.
