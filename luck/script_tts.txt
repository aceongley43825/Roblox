In this town, everyone has a luck percentage floating above their head. The higher your percentage, the luckier you are. But there's one person whose percentage makes absolutely no sense. The mayor has 1,000% luck. Nobody knows how that's possible. Last week, my best friend went from 87% luck to 2% overnight. And by the afternoon, he had disappeared. I started checking everyone's percentages. People were losing luck every single day. Then I noticed something terrifying. Every time someone's luck dropped, the mayor's went up. I knew something wasn't right, so I went straight to his office. I demanded to know what was happening. He finally admitted the truth. Years ago, he discovered a way to steal people's luck. Every time someone's percentage dropped, their luck was transferred to him. He had been doing it for years. But he needed everyone's luck to reach 0% to complete his plan. I looked outside. Everyone's percentage was now 0%. Suddenly, my phone buzzed. A notification appeared: "LUCK TRANSFER COMPLETE." I looked at the mayor. He started laughing. I asked him what was so funny. He said, "You actually thought I was stealing luck for myself?" Suddenly, the sky turned completely black. A giant percentage appeared above the clouds. 0% LUCK. The mayor smiled and said: "I wasn't stealing your luck to save myself..." "I was collecting enough luck to protect us from what's coming." Then every phone in town received the same message: "THE UNLUCKY ONE HAS ARRIVED." And for the first time in history... The mayor's percentage dropped to zero.
