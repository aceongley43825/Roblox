In this town, every night at exactly 3 AM, all the streetlights turn red. And there's one rule everyone follows. Never go outside when the lights turn red. My parents told me it was because of dangerous criminals who roam the streets at night. But one night, I decided to look out of my bedroom window. At exactly 3 AM, every streetlight turned red. At first, everything seemed normal. The street was empty, and all the houses were dark. Until I noticed a man standing under the streetlight across the road. He was staring straight at my window. Then he waved at me. And I realized it was my dad. But my dad was supposed to be asleep down the hall. Before I could do anything, every front door on the street opened at once. All the grown-ups tiptoed outside in their pajamas and walked toward the park. So I broke the rule. I sneaked out the back door and followed them. At the park, I couldn't believe my eyes. My mom was flying down the big slide. Mr. Hendricks next door was spinning on the merry-go-round. And my teacher was hanging upside down from the monkey bars. They weren't hiding from criminals. They were playing. Then my dad turned around and saw me. He said kids hog the playground all day, so 3 AM is the only time grown-ups get a turn. And he made me promise to keep it secret. So now, when the lights turn red, I sneak out too, as the lookout. My parents were right after all. There really are dangerous criminals roaming the streets at 3 AM. And now I'm one of them.
