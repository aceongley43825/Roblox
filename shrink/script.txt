In my town, every time you tell a lie, you shrink. Tell the truth, and you grow back. My name is Max, and I was the shortest kid in school. Not because I lied. Because my brother Tyler lied about me. He told everyone I cheated on the math test. The whole class laughed. But Tyler didn't shrink. Not even a little. That's weird, I thought. Everyone shrinks when they lie. So I followed him home. He went into the garage and opened a secret box. Inside was a glowing golden ruler. Every time he lied, he touched it, and stayed the same size. I grabbed the ruler and ran. The next day at school, Tyler stood up and said, Max is a cheater. Suddenly he shrank. And shrank. Until he was the size of a pencil. The whole class gasped. Then I said, I never cheated. And I grew taller than everyone. Tyler squeaked, Okay, okay, I lied! And he grew back. Then the teacher looked at me and asked, Max, where did you get that ruler? I said, I found it. And I shrank.
