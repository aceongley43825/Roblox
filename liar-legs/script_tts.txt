In this town, every time you tell a lie, your legs get longer. The only way to shrink back down is to tell the truth. That's why the mailman is twelve feet tall, and why the mayor has to duck under the stoplights. My name is Sam, and I had never told a single lie, so my legs were normal. Then one morning, my little sister Ruby kicked her soccer ball into Mom's favorite vase. She grabbed my arm and begged me, Please don't tell. So when Mom asked, Sam, do you know who did this, I said, No idea. My legs shot up two feet. Mom stared at me. Ruby stared at me. I said, It's just a growth spurt. Another foot. By dinner, I was bumping my head on the ceiling fan, and that night I had to sleep with my feet out the window. The next morning, I told Mom the truth. My legs shrank back down. Mom hugged me. Then she turned to Ruby and asked, Is that true? Ruby said, Nope. And her legs grew so long, she stepped right over the house.
