In this town, everybody walks backwards. Nobody remembers why. My mom walks backwards to the grocery store. The mailman walks backwards with a bag full of letters. Even our dog walks backwards, and he is not happy about it. Except me. I walk forwards. Everybody thinks I'm weird. At school, they call me Front Face, and nobody wants to sit next to me at lunch. Then came the big summer parade. The whole town was marching backwards down Main Street, with the mayor in front, smiling and waving at nobody. But I was the only one facing the right way, so I was the only one who saw it. A giant hole, right in the middle of the road. Nobody else could see it, because they were walking straight towards it backwards. So I ran ahead of the parade and yelled STOP! The whole town froze. They turned around, and saw the hole for the first time. The mayor was one step away from falling in. The next morning, he gave me a gold medal, and now everybody in town walks forwards. Everybody, except the dog.
